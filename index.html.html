02 / MY APPROACH

Structured thinking.
Curious by default.

I try to make the problem clear before jumping to a solution—and make trade-offs visible along the way.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the user

Clarify who the problem affects, what they are trying to do, and where friction appears.

02 / EXPLORE

Look for evidence

Review alternatives, competitor patterns, and assumptions that need to be tested.

03 / SHAPE

Make it tangible

Turn findings into a focused scope, clear flows, wireframes, and useful requirements.

04 / LEARN

Improve the next step

Identify what still needs validation and what evidence would help decide what to do next.
